Doot Support

Quick answers, and a real person if you need one.

Something not working, or an idea for Doot? Email hello@zerostarapps.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

How do I add a kid?

On the home screen, tap Add Kid, type their name, and pick a color and avatar. They start with a ready-made morning and evening routine.

How do I change or remove a kid?

Press and hold the kid's name on the home screen and choose Edit Kid. You can change their name, color or avatar, or tap Delete Kid at the bottom.

How do I change the tasks in a routine?

Open a kid's routine and tap Add Task at the end of the list. To rename, change or delete a task, press and hold it and choose Edit Task. Changes apply to that routine every day from then on.

How do I switch between morning and evening?

Doot opens on the morning routine before noon and the evening routine after. To switch, swipe left or right, or tap Morning or Evening at the top.

Do the checkmarks reset?

Yes. Each day starts fresh automatically, so there's nothing to clear.

Does Doot sync between phones?

Not yet. Doot keeps everything on the device you use it on. Sync between a family's devices is on our list for a future version.

Is my family's information private?

Yes. Doot collects no data and has no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
