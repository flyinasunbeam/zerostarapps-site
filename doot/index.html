Doot

Morning and bedtime routines kids can check off themselves.

Brushing teeth, getting dressed, tidying up: kids know what to do, they just don't remember yet. Doot turns each routine into big, friendly cards they tap as they go, with a little celebration at the end.

  • Morning and evening routines for each kid, ready to go
  • Add, edit or remove tasks to fit your family
  • A name, color and avatar for every kid
  • Starts fresh every day, automatically
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone
